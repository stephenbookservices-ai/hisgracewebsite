Nursery · Primary · Secondary

A school where every child is known by name

We teach nursery, primary and secondary pupils under one roof, so a child can grow in the same caring community for years.

Lessons are guided by high academic standards and by Christian values: honesty, respect, discipline and service to others.

Life at His Grace and Power

Three stages, one family

Nursery

Play, songs, early reading and counting in a safe, warm setting that builds confidence and curiosity.

Primary

Solid English, mathematics and science foundations, plus creative arts, moral instruction and good study habits.

Secondary

Subject teaching and exam preparation that equips students for further education and responsible adult life.

Clear reports, every term

Parents should never have to guess how their child is doing. We track progress through the term and share it in writing.

  • Termly report card with continuous assessment, exam scores, grades and teacher’s comments
  • Remarks on conduct, punctuality and attendance
  • Parent–teacher meetings to discuss progress and next steps
  • Prompt feedback if a child needs extra help

How to join the school

  1. Enquire

    Message us on WhatsApp or send an email with your child’s name and age.

  2. Visit

    Come to the school, meet our teachers and see the classrooms.

  3. Assess

    Your child takes a short placement assessment so we can choose the right class.

  4. Register

    Complete the forms, submit your documents and begin the term.

Which classes do you offer?

We run nursery, primary and secondary classes in one school.

How do I get my child’s report?

Report cards are issued at the end of each term. You can also ask the school about progress at any time.

What documents do I need?

Bring your child’s birth certificate, passport photographs and the most recent report card if they are transferring.

How much are school fees?

Fees depend on the class. Message us on WhatsApp for the current fee schedule.

Admissions are open

Visit the school, meet our teachers and find the right class for your child. Message us on WhatsApp to get started.
